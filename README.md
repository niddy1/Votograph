## Votograph
A Ruby on Rails app where users upload pictures and create head-to-head image polls for other users to vote on.
Votograph is mobile-responsive, with a design built using Materialize.

## User Story
- User signs up and logs in, then uploads pictures to their own profile.
- User picks any two of their pictures and creates a poll from them.
- Other users see two pictures side by side and click the one they prefer.
- After voting, a pop-up confirms the vote and shows the percentage of voters who agree.
- A poll's creator can't vote on their own poll.
- Poll creation is blocked until two pictures are selected.
- "My Polls" shows each poll's vote counts and percentages, and polls can be deleted with one click (via an AJAX call).
